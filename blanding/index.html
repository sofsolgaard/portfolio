About me

Hi, I'm Sofie

I'm 20, from Denmark, and I spend most of my year outside. On a bike, on skis, on foot or by the sea. And I share it the way it actually is.

I also love slow living: saunas, cold swims, breakfast outside and making the small moments count. That calm side is just as much me as the long days in the mountains.

My content is calm, honest and functional. Nature first, real products in real moments, no filters. I love inspiring others to get outside, especially more young women.
